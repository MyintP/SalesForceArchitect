Career path to Salesforce Architect

A structured roadmap from platform fundamentals to the architect track — built from the official Trailhead architect trail and cross-referenced against Salesforce Dictionary's certification and learning resources.

SCALE: foundations before certifications DRAWN FOR: Phil Myint

01 — Roadmap

1

Discover the architect role

~20 min

Orient to what a Salesforce Architect actually does before investing in the platform mechanics.

  • What is a Salesforce Architect? — role & career pathway overview
  • Salesforce Architect: Quick Look badge — responsibilities and core competencies
  • How the Architect Role Is Evolving in the Agentic AI Era — where the role is heading
  • Think Like an Architect — expert analysis of real design scenarios
2

Platform foundations

~5.5 h

Org structure, data model, and the security model — the vocabulary every later stage assumes.

  • Salesforce Platform Basics trail — orgs, objects, fields, records
  • Data model & relationships — lookup vs master-detail, junction objects, schema builder
  • Security & sharing model — profiles, permission sets, org-wide defaults, sharing rules
3

Automation & declarative tools

~2.5 h

Flow is the most likely concrete example a hiring panel or exam will probe.

  • Flow Builder fundamentals — screen flows, record-triggered flows, core logic
  • When to use Flow vs Apex — a framing you can argue without being a developer
4

Integration architecture

~3 h

Where prior enterprise architecture experience transfers most directly.

  • REST & SOAP APIs on the platform — how Salesforce exposes and consumes web services
  • Platform Events & event-driven patterns — the platform's take on event-driven architecture
  • External identity & SSO — maps closely to Entra ID / IAM work
5

Multi-cloud & governance

~2 h

How the clouds relate to each other, and how architecture governance is exercised on-platform.

  • Sales, Service & Experience Cloud overview — what each is for, discussion-level fluency
  • Architecture governance on Salesforce — design reviews, quality gates, target-state roadmaps
6

Architect the agentic enterprise

~5 h · +2,100 pts

The newest, heaviest module on the official trail — where the role is expanding fastest.

  • IT Architecture of the Agentic Enterprise — system design for agent-driven orgs
  • The Agent Development Lifecycle
  • Agentic Patterns and Implementation with Agentforce
  • Data 360 Architecture — unified data & integration foundation
  • Technical Debt badge — +2,000 pts, the trail's largest single badge
  • Agent Customization: Quick Look badge
7

Certification track

ongoing

Not a sprint — layer credentials as depth in each domain solidifies.

01Administrator (ADM-201) — the foundation cert nearly every path assumes
02Platform App Builder — declarative build depth
03Architect Journey trailmix — Data Architecture, Sharing & Visibility, Platform Dev, Integration Architecture, Identity & Access Management
04Application / System Architect — domain architect designations
05Certified Technical Architect (CTA) — the board-reviewed capstone
